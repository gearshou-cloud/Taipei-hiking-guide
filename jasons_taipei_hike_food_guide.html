Local food detours

No hike required. These are worth saving for another morning or evening.

🌙 Shilin 士林

More touristy, but still fun for a first Taiwan trip. Don't sit down for one big meal — make it a snack crawl.

Shilin Night Market 士林夜市Try 2–4 small things: pepper bun, fried chicken, oyster omelet, sweet potato balls or aiyu jelly.STREET-FOOD NIGHT
Jason's rule for eating solo in TaipeiMarkets, noodle shops, rice bowls and street food are your friends. If the menu is built around a whole chicken, several stir-fries and a big soup, save it for a group.
